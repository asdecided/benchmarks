The Lighthouse Keeper

Index Poem Decoys

The keeper climbs the spiral stairs each evening to light the great lamp before the fog swallows the rocky shore.

Ships far out at sea steer by that single point of light and trust it more than any modern chart.

When morning comes the keeper trims the wick writes the night into the logbook and sleeps while gulls argue outside.

These footer words are decoys and must never be counted by the badge.
