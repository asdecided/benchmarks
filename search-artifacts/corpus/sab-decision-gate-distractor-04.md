# Compliance Catalogue D

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The fourth catalogue groups assurance meetings. Archive status, retention
exceptions, lifecycle ownership, and expiry incidents can be agenda topics, but
the catalogue contains no operative storage rule.

## Decision

Use catalogue D to organise assurance meetings only.

## Consequences

Meeting metadata never becomes implementation policy.
