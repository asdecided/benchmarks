# Enterprise Readiness

## Status

Planned

## Context

Larger customers ask for isolation guarantees, audit evidence, and device sign-in for shared displays before they will roll Meridian out beyond a pilot.

## Outcomes

- A regulated customer can pass its compliance review on Meridian.
- Shared displays sign in without shared passwords.

## Initiatives

- Ship query audit logging and its retention controls.
- Ship schema isolation hardening and the support-role grant flow.
- Ship device-flow sign-in for displays.

## Success Measures

- Two enterprise contracts close on the strength of the audit trail.
