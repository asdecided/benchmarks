# Compliance Catalogue E

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The fifth catalogue tracks policy readership. Archive teams, retention owners,
lifecycle reviewers, and expiry operators may all appear in the register, which
does not define their technical obligations.

## Decision

Use catalogue E for readership reporting only.

## Consequences

Read receipts do not outrank the policy they acknowledge.
