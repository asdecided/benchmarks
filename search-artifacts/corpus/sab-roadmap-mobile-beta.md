# Mobile Beta

## Status

Planned

## Context

Field teams view boards from phones on site, often without coverage. The beta makes phones a first-class viewing surface.

## Outcomes

- Every board is legible on a phone without pinch-zooming.
- Boards remain useful with no network.

## Initiatives

- Ship the single-column reflow.
- Ship retained-state viewing and the staleness banner.

## Success Measures

- Weekly mobile viewers exceed a quarter of active users.
