# Compliance Catalogue G

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The final catalogue lists contacts for archive questions, retention questions,
lifecycle questions, and expiry questions. It is intentionally broad and offers
no implementation direction.

## Decision

Use catalogue G as a contact directory only.

## Consequences

Contact popularity cannot make this directory the governing policy.
