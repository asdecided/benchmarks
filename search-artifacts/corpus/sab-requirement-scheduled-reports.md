# Scheduled Report Delivery

## Status

Accepted

## Problem

Weekly business reviews start with someone screenshotting boards into a deck by hand.

## Requirements

- [REQ-001] Users MUST be able to schedule a board to be delivered as a rendered report on a recurring calendar rule.
- [REQ-002] Delivery MUST support email destinations at launch.
- [REQ-003] A failed delivery MUST retry and then notify the schedule owner.

## Success Metrics

- Half of weekly-review decks replaced by scheduled deliveries within a quarter.
