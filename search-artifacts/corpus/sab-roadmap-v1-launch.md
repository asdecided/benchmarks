# Meridian v1 Launch

## Status

Planned

## Context

The v1 launch bundles the capabilities a small analytics team needs to adopt Meridian as its daily board surface.

## Outcomes

- A team can ingest from its main sources, build boards, and trust the figures.
- Boards are shareable inside a workspace with real roles.

## Initiatives

- Ship the metrics store and rollup pipeline.
- Ship the query builder canvas and the desktop grid.
- Ship sharing permissions.

## Success Measures

- Ten design-partner teams use Meridian boards in their weekly review.
