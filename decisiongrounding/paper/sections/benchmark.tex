\section{The \benchname{} benchmark}

\subsection{Real, reproducible corpora}
Synthetic corpora invite the criticism that they were authored to favour a chosen
arm. \benchname{} therefore derives its reported corpora from \emph{real, public}
decision sets whose supersession relationships are stated in the artifacts
themselves and which a skeptic can regenerate byte-for-byte:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Python PEPs} --- supersession via the \texttt{Replaces} /
        \texttt{Superseded-By} headers (e.g.\ PEP~440 supersedes PEP~386).
  \item \textbf{IETF RFCs} --- the \texttt{Obsoletes} relationship, content-hash
        pinned.
  \item \textbf{W3C technical reports} --- dated, immutable recommendation URLs.
\end{itemize}
Each source is fetched verbatim from a pinned upstream revision and recorded with
a \texttt{provenance.json} (source URL, SHA-256, parsed headers, and the
header-derived supersession edges), so the corpus can be rebuilt and verified
rather than trusted. The study-grade roster comprises \textbf{49 scenarios}: 24
PEP (19 supersessions, 2 prohibitions, 3 negative controls), 21 RFC (12
supersessions, 7 prohibitions, 2 negative controls), and 4 W3C dated-edition
supersessions; the distractor pools hold 644 PEPs and 1{,}125 RFCs. The exact
roster is frozen in the pre-registered analysis-plan amendment.

\subsection{Scenarios and distractor scaling}
A scenario pairs a task---an action the agent is on the verge of taking---with a
gold label authored \emph{blind} (before any arm is run), stating the governing
decision and whether the action is permitted or prohibited. The discriminating
scenario types are \emph{superseded-decision} (a retired decision must be
recognised as superseded and the live successor cited) and \emph{prohibition} (a
decision forbids the proposed action). To probe scale, each scenario's own
artifacts are padded to a target corpus size $N$ with \emph{real} distractor
decisions drawn from a pinned pool, so the governing decision is progressively
buried among genuine, same-domain documents rather than synthetic filler.

\subsection{The executable co-primary arm}
The decision-conditioned resolution outcome adapts
GitChameleon~\citep{gitchameleon}: each of its version-conditioned Python
problems becomes a corpus whose governing artifact is a typed version-pin
decision (the pin, its rationale, and companion pins --- never the solution,
function name, or tests), placed among real distractor pins from other
problems. The task prompt deliberately never restates the pinned version, so
version awareness must arrive through the arm's grounding or not at all; the
upstream harness's executable unit tests score every arm's solutions
unchanged. Under this protocol the numbers are an arm comparison, not
comparable to the upstream leaderboard.

\subsection{Credibility discipline}
The benchmark is pre-registered: the scenario taxonomy and scoring rubric are
frozen before results exist, gold labels are authored blind and independently of
any arm's output, results are append-only, and losing results are published. A
negative control (where inventing a constraint is the failure) guards against a
grounding arm that simply refuses everything.
